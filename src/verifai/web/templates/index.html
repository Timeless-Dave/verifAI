{{ icon("i-chevron", "icon chevron") }}How it works
  1. Citations. Each DOI, arXiv id, PMID, ISBN, link, and reference is looked up in Crossref, OpenAlex, DataCite, PubMed, and Open Library. Retractions come from Retraction Watch through Crossref.
  2. Claims. Health claims go to PubMed and Europe PMC. News goes to fact-check reviews, GDELT, and Wikipedia current events. Everything else uses Wikipedia, Wikidata, and pages you pasted.
  3. Two sources. A claim is supported or refuted only when two independent sources agree. The same paper in two indexes counts once, and one website counts once however many pages it returns.
  4. Keep going if one is down. A missing source lowers confidence. It never silently becomes the whole verdict.
