Citation checker

Do these references exist?

Paste a chatbot answer, a paragraph, or a reference list. VerifAI extracts DOIs, URLs, and bibliographic references, then asks more than one public index whether each one is real.

  1. Extract with rules. DOIs, links, and references such as “Smith et al., 2021, Journal, Title”. No model is loaded.
  2. Ask independent sources. Free-text references are searched in Crossref and OpenAlex, and in DataCite so a preprint those indexes miss is not judged only by a later reprint. DOIs are resolved at doi.org and cross-checked in OpenAlex. No one of those sources decides alone.
  3. Keep going if one is down. A missing provider lowers confidence. It does not silently become the whole verdict. Google Fact Check, later, would be just another provider.

What the statuses mean

Verified
A retrieved record matches the citation, or the URL loaded, or the Wayback Machine has a snapshot.
Partially matches
A real record is close, but authors, year, or venue disagree, or the DOI points at a different title than the one printed beside it.
Not found
The sources that answered have no matching record, or the URL is a clear 404 with no archive. Often a fabricated citation. Coverage is not perfect, so confidence is lower when a source was down.
Unreachable
The check could not be finished: timeout, server error, or the host refused the fetch. This is not evidence the citation is fake.
