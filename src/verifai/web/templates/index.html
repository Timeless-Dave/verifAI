Claim checker

Do these references exist?

Paste a claim, a chatbot answer, or a link. VerifAI sends each claim to the matching checker, then shows one verdict with every source linked. A verdict other than unverified needs two independent sources that agree.

  1. Pick a checker. Health claims go to PubMed and Europe PMC. News and politics go to fact-check reviews, GDELT, and Wikipedia current events. Other claims use Wikipedia, Wikidata, and pages you pasted. Citations still go to Crossref and OpenAlex.
  2. Ask independent sources. Google Fact Check, when a key is set, counts as one source even if it returns several reviews. The same paper in two indexes counts once. No one of those sources decides alone.
  3. Keep going if one is down. A missing provider lowers confidence. It does not silently become the whole verdict. Without a local model, pages are listed but not judged.

What the statuses mean

Verified
A retrieved record matches the citation, or the URL loaded, or the Wayback Machine has a snapshot.
Partially matches
A real record is close, but authors, year, or venue disagree, or the DOI points at a different title than the one printed beside it.
Not found
The sources that answered have no matching record, or the URL is a clear 404 with no archive. Often a fabricated citation. Coverage is not perfect, so confidence is lower when a source was down.
Unreachable
The check could not be finished: timeout, server error, or the host refused the fetch. This is not evidence the citation is fake.
