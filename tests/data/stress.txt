Here's a quick overview of where modern AI came from, with sources.

Backpropagation was popularized by Rumelhart, D. E., Hinton, G. E., & Williams, R. J. (1986). Learning representations by back-propagating errors. Nature, 323, 533–536. doi:10.1038/323533a0

Contrary to popular belief, backpropagation was first described by Isaac Newton in the Principia.
